// why I built this

I was running a TopstepX combine on ES and kept second-guessing myself in the first hour of the session — sizing up after a loss, skipping a stop, staying in a trade past my own exit signal.

The combine account rules aren't complicated. My execution was the problem. So I automated the routine I was already doing by hand, with the discipline part built in instead of relying on willpower at 8:15am.

This doesn't guarantee a win rate. It executes a set of rules. Whether those rules perform in your account depends on market conditions, your settings, and factors neither of us can predict.
